The trace

A numbered walk through real files. This is the highest-value block in the template — a newcomer learns more from one verified end-to-end trace than from any amount of description.

  1. internal/cli/example.go:42 What happens here, in one sentence.

The pattern to copy

Name the convention explicitly and point at the exemplar the learner should imitate. "Implementation patterns" is an explicit goal of this course — do not leave the convention implicit and hope they infer it.

Trap

State the wrong belief first, then the reality, then the evidence at path:line. Traps are the highest-retention content in a lesson because they attach to a prediction error.
